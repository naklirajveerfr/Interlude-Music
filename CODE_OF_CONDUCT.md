# Interlude Music — Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Interlude Music is an open-source project built by and for people who love music and technology.

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment where everyone can contribute, share ideas, report bugs, and collaborate.

Whether you're a developer, designer, translator, or user, you're welcome here.

## Expected Behavior

All community members are expected to:

- **Be respectful:** Treat others with kindness and respect, regardless of their experience or background.
- **Be constructive:** Provide helpful feedback and criticism without personal attacks.
- **Be collaborative:** Encourage discussions, share knowledge, and help others when possible.
- **Respect different opinions:** Disagreements are natural, but discussions should remain civil and productive.
- **Give proper credit:** Acknowledge the contributions, ideas, and work of others.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or hate speech.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately offensive comments.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouragement of violence.
- Spam, trolling, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Sharing someone else's private information without permission.
- Any other behavior that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all official Interlude Music community spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Code reviews and project-related conversations.
- Other official community platforms associated with Interlude Music.

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate discretion.

Please avoid publicly sharing sensitive information about incidents or individuals.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Depending on the severity of the violation, actions may include:

1. A private warning.
2. Removal or editing of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Permanent removal from project community spaces.

Serious violations may result in immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

## Our Goal

Interlude Music is a passion project, and its community should reflect that passion.

We're here to build something great, learn from one another, and make music listening better for everyone.

**Be respectful. Be helpful. Enjoy the music.**
